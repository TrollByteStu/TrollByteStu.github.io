Idea journal

Every idea for the game, from the moment we thought of it: what it is, where it stands, and what happened to it. Ideas grow, change and get cancelled, and that history stays here. Decisions and votes are on their own page: Decisions.

Being built

Adopted

A tank you build yourself

The tank is not one model. You build it from floors, walls and armour, like a base in Ark or Conan Exiles, and you can run around inside it while it drives.

First thought of: 2026 Oct 5
Partly built

Saved designs, and student tanks as enemies

Save a tank you built with a name, author, description and picture. Good student designs can later become enemy tanks and buildings in the world.

First thought of: 2026 Oct 17
Partly built

Getting between floors

Stairs work. Hatches come later, and only if they really open and close. A paternoster lift is a favourite.

Skins and faction looks

The engine's brain

Give the tank its personality with an LLM, using the Player2 plugin made over the summer. It reacts to being shot, to hills and to repairs.

Open questions and votes

Open

How should decor turn?

Q turns pieces around the up axis, which is wrong for rivets on a wall. One turn key, two, or more snap points? To decide early, before there are 200 armour pieces.

First thought of: 2026 Oct 17

The convention arcade rig

Proposed

Proposed

Damage you can see and repair

Every wall section has three states: intact, wounded and destroyed. Wounded parts look bent and dented, and the crew repairs them.

First thought of: 2026 Oct
Proposed

Turrets everywhere

A single gun turret in the middle looks too modern. The dream is a fantasy tank with turrets all over, and turrets you can take off and swap.

First thought of: 2026 Oct
Proposed

Tank tracks: real links up close, a moving belt far away

The tank drives on 6–8 hidden wheels. The tracks you see are just for show: link models up close, a scrolling texture from far away.

First thought of: 2026 Oct 7
Proposed

Movement mods you equip

Wall-running, double jump, grappling hook, jump jet: students build them as mods, and a player equips one at a time.

First thought of: 2026 Oct 9
Proposed

First thought of: 2026 Oct 17
Proposed
