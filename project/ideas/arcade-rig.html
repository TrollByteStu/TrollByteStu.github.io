The convention arcade rig

Waiting for a vote First thought of: Pitch

Five people crew one tank on a bench at an expo, each at a station. Build it now, or later?

From the pitch: five people sit back to back on a bench with a big screen overhead, each at a station (Gunner, Engineer, Navigator, Radio, Therapist). The queue becomes the tutorial. A side project for the hardware-minded: 3D printing, Arduino, haptics. One of the four forks for the student vote.
