A Workshop level

Proposed First thought of: 2026 Oct 17

Maybe designs can only be saved and loaded in a special Workshop level, not out in the world.

Saving and loading tank designs is a planning tool. In time it might only work in a Workshop level, so it can't be used as a cheat in the middle of a fight.
