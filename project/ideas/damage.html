Cranky Engines · Idea journal

Damage you can see and repair

Proposed First thought of: 2026 Oct

Every wall section has three states: intact, wounded and destroyed. Wounded parts look bent and dented, and the crew repairs them.

The tank is built from sections, so damage can be per section. Each one has three states: intact, wounded and destroyed. That gives the crew something to fix in the middle of a fight, and it sets the pattern for later stations like engines and workbenches.

A suggestion from another teacher for the wounded look: one armour model with eight panels that can be bent by shape keys, so a single model gives many different dents. There is a static-mesh version of this to test first.

History

  • 2026 Oct: Idea: three states per wall section.
  • 2026 Oct 17: Suggestion from another teacher: one model, many dents. Written down to test.
  • 2026 Oct 15: Planned as the next big step after multiplayer: a cannon, a second tank to shoot at, and walls that take damage.
