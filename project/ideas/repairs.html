Repairs: in the field, in a workshop, or while you rest?

Partly built First thought of: 2026 Oct

When a piece is destroyed, can you fix it in the field? Only in a workshop? Or does the tank repair itself while it rests at a base? Repair in the field is built now; the rest is still open.

Repair is part of the first milestone, and it decides a lot about how a fight feels. Questions from our debate (week of 2026 Oct 19):

  • In the field: can a completely destroyed piece be repaired out in the world, or only damaged ones?
  • Workshops: what if some bases have workshops? Can "locked" or broken pieces only be repaired there?
  • Resting: if you drive in and the tank is lifted up on a hoist, does it repair slowly just by being there, while you rest, shop or find the next mission?
  • Getting stuck: if your turrets are destroyed and can't be repaired, could that stop you finishing a mission and leave you stuck?

We will try the answers out when there is gameplay to test them in.

History

  • 2026 Oct 23: First test: a combined armour piece repairs itself slowly (a few health points a second), going back through its looks: curled, dented, whole. Never back through the torn-to-bits stage.
  • Week of 2026 Oct 19: A long debate about repairs: in the field, in workshops, while resting at a base, and whether destroyed turrets could leave you stuck. No decision yet.
  • 2026 Oct 27: Built: a welding torch. Hold R to repair the armour near you; it wears out as you use it.
  • 2026 Oct 30: Built: every piece on the tank has health, breaks at 0 and stops working, and the welding torch brings it back.
  • 2026 Oct 31: Built: repairing a completely destroyed piece costs its materials again. In the workshops everything is free.
