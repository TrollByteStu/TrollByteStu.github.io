The crew headset

Partly built First thought of: 2026 Nov 8

Every crew member wears a steampunk headset with a monocle, an earpiece, a microphone and a head lamp. One piece of kit solves four problems.

Four problems, one headset

  1. Friend, foe or civilian? Tank crews wear the headset; civilians don't. You can tell who is who at a glance.
  2. Too many lamps. A tank full of rooms would need dozens of lights, and every light costs. Instead, each crew member brings their own light. Aim, and the beam gets tighter and brighter.
  3. A place for the HUD. The monocle lens is where on-screen information can come from.
  4. Team talk. The earpiece and microphone are how the crew talks to each other, and maybe to the tank.

History

  • 2026 Nov 8: First idea, from a concept sheet. The first headset was modelled and put on the player, with a lamp that tightens when you aim. Not yet: turning the lamp on and off, and headsets only on crews.
