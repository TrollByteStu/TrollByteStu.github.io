Characters of other sizes

Parked First thought of: 2026 Oct 9

Small and big races. Climbing now works at other sizes, but the hands do not quite meet the edge. Parked: the prototype uses one size.

We tested characters at 100, 172 and 250 cm. After three fixes, climbs end on the surface at every size, but the hands don't meet the edge exactly at the extremes. That is accepted: if races come, keep them closer to 172 cm. The prototype uses the normal 172 cm character, and the tank's doors and corridors are measured for it.

History

  • 2026 Oct 9: Tested and fixed as far as reasonable, then parked.
