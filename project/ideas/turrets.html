Turrets everywhere

Proposed First thought of: 2026 Oct

A single gun turret in the middle looks too modern. The dream is a fantasy tank with turrets all over, and turrets you can take off and swap.

Real modern tanks have one turret in the middle. Ours should look like fantasy, closer to the many-gunned tanks of Warhammer 40,000. Because the tank is modular, a turret should be a piece like any other: take one off, slot another in.
