Quests: shared and secret

Partly built First thought of: 2026 Nov 1

People in the world give jobs made of steps. Crew quests belong to the tank and everyone on it; personal quests are secret.

A quest is a list of steps and a reward. The steps:

  • Go to a place or a person (with a marker).
  • Find someone or something (no marker: you have to look).
  • Talk to someone.
  • Bring items, or deliver them to someone.
  • Destroy enemies.

They combine into small stories: "Go to the village, find the postman, talk to him." Crew quests go in the tank's shared log, so anyone on the crew can do the steps. Personal quests go in your own log, which nobody else can see, so a crew member can have a secret job: a traitor. Quests are made by hand by students with the Quest Maker, and later an AI game master can write new ones in the same format.

History

  • 2026 Nov 1: Idea and first version: quest steps, crew and personal quest logs, a quest giver, the quest log on J and markers on screen. Tested with a postman quest and a crew quest. The Quest Maker tool lets students make quests from a form.
