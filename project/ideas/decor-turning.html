How should decor turn?

Open First thought of: 2026 Oct 17

Q turns pieces around the up axis, which is wrong for rivets on a wall. One turn key, two, or more snap points? To decide early, before there are 200 armour pieces.

Decor (rivets, pipes) snaps to points on walls and floors. Today Q turns everything around the up axis, which looks wrong on a wall. Possible fixes:

  • Each piece says how it may turn, per axis (half turns, quarter turns, or eighths for something like a rust patch).
  • Two turn keys, one for each direction.
  • More snap points instead of turning, for example rivets going up the armour as well as across.

This must be decided early, because every armour piece will carry these snap points. To talk through with the students and the 3D teachers.
