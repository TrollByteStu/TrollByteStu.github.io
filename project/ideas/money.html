Power crystals: a money for each faction

Partly built First thought of: 2026 Nov 1

Money is charged power crystals in brass rings. The city's reactor is what gives them their value, and every faction has its own.

The Empire's money, the first one:

  • Spark (blue): the Imperial Power Standard, worth 1.
  • Click (green): the Imperial Kilo Standard, 1 000 sparks.
  • Mulah (red): the Imperial Mega Standard, a million sparks.
  • Bingo (violet): the Imperial Bonanza, a billion sparks.

Crystals are items like any other: they sit in your backpack or the tank, and can be dropped and looted. Nobody counts change: when you pay or get paid, the crystals are remade in as few pieces as possible. Later, most factions get their own money with its own name and nickname, and maybe bartering on top. A player's own reactor is not planned for a long while: for now, "the city has a reactor".

History

  • 2026 Nov 1: Idea and first version: the four Imperial crystals, change made by itself, and shops with their own lists, prices and stock. Commissions for the crystal models and a shop counter went to the 3D team.
