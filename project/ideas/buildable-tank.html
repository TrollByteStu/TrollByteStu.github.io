A tank you build yourself

Adopted First thought of: 2026 Oct 5

The tank is not one model. You build it from floors, walls and armour, like a base in Ark or Conan Exiles, and you can run around inside it while it drives.

The tank is the crew's home, so the crew should build it. Pieces snap to a grid of 3 × 3 × 3 metre cells: floors, straight walls, armour that slopes inwards, corners, track housings, stairs and decor. The building style is closer to Ark or Conan Exiles than to Space Engineers: quick to snap together, not a puzzle of tiny blocks.

Ideas around it that are still open: a thick, mechanical bottom floor; a central column that jacks the tank up like a piston while you build; and whether building happens in the normal view or in a top-down, strategy-style view (the view changes how players think about what they build).

History

  • 2026 Oct 5: The goal is set: a modular tank you can run around in while it moves.
  • 2026 Oct 12: Grid decided: 3 × 3 × 3 m cells, placed in half-cell steps.
  • 2026 Oct 12: Built: floors, walls, sloped armour and a test core with a piston.
  • 2026 Oct 13: Built: build menu, track housings, building on a tank that drives.
  • 2026 Oct 17: Built: decor, stairs and half floor tiles.
