Tank tracks: real links up close, a moving belt far away

Proposed First thought of: 2026 Oct 7

The tank drives on 6–8 hidden wheels. The tracks you see are just for show: link models up close, a scrolling texture from far away.

Simulating every track link with physics is far too expensive, and no shipped game we found does it. So the tank drives on six to eight wheels that nobody sees, and the tracks are drawn on top.

  • Close up: one link model, repeated along a path around the wheels. The links move by the distance the tank has driven, so the links on the ground stand still while the tank rolls over them, like real tracks.
  • Far away: a simple belt with a texture that scrolls by the same distance.

The tracks system is probably too complex as a student task. Students can make the parts it uses: the link, the wheels and the belt texture.

History

  • 2026 Oct 7: Researched how other games do it; recommendation written.
  • 2026 Oct 9: Decided: 6–8 simulated wheels, tracks only for show.
  • Later: Nothing built yet.
