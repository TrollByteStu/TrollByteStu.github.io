Getting between floors

Partly built First thought of: 2026 Oct 17

Stairs work. Hatches come later, and only if they really open and close. A paternoster lift is a favourite.

The tank has several storeys, so the crew needs a way up and down. The options we talked about:

  • Stairs (1.5 m wide, one storey, banisters made of blocks): built and tested. Walking up them works.
  • Hatches: later, and only if they really open and close. "Battle stations, close all hatches!" Static things that look like they should move annoy players.
  • Ladders and ropes: need new animations.
  • A steampunk lift: a moving box inside a tank that rolls over rough ground and gets shot at. Very steampunk.
  • A paternoster (an open lift that never stops, like the one at Christiansborg), up one side of the central column and down the other. A favourite.

Buildings in the world may use different ones than the tank.

History

  • 2026 Oct 17: Options talked through and written down.
  • 2026 Oct 17: Built: stairs and a half-size floor tile next to them.
