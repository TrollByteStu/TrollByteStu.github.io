Cranky Engines · Idea journal

Saved designs, and student tanks as enemies

Partly built First thought of: 2026 Oct 17

Save a tank you built with a name, author, description and picture. Good student designs can later become enemy tanks and buildings in the world.

Students build tanks (and later buildings) in the game and save them as designs. The best ones get promoted to the game's own content and placed in the world by a spawner, so enemy tanks and points of interest can come straight from the class.

For now, saving and loading only works while you drive the tank, in single player. Later it may only work in a special Workshop level.

History

  • 2026 Oct 17: Planned: designs with name, author, description and skins per piece.
  • 2026 Oct 17: Built: the design library (L in the control box) with categories and a picture taken from the driving camera.
  • Later: Not built: promoting a design to the game, and the spawner.
