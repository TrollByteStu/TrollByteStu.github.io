Movement mods you equip

Proposed First thought of: 2026 Oct 9

Wall-running, double jump, grappling hook, jump jet: students build them as mods, and a player equips one at a time.

There are many tutorials for adding movement to the character. Instead of adding them all, students build them as mods that a player equips one at a time, so one mod cannot break another. Every mod has to pass the same tests (rolling corridors, height pads) before it is kept.

It also follows a design idea: so much has moved from the player to the tank that the player needs things the tank cannot do, like going into buildings and parkour.
