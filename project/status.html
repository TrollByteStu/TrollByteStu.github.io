Where we are

What is built and working today, by milestone. Updated every week. Last updated: 2026 Nov 1.

Milestone 1: a working prototype in greybox

One test level: from one village to the next, getting attacked on the way. Everything is a placeholder. Nothing is pretty and nothing is polished; everything is there to test. The point is to get a sense of what it would be like to play this in a game world, and then to ask ourselves and others: is this game worthy of existing, or is our time better used elsewhere?

PlayersWorkingThe GASP character: running, jumping, crouching, climbing. Play spawns the Mover character. Footsteps sound like the ground you are on.
ParkourWorkingClimb and vault on any mesh, also onto a tank that is driving.
Tank controlsWorkingPress E at any control panel to drive: W/S, A/D. One driver per tank. Tracks, engine gears and sounds that follow the driving. Two versions to choose between (simulated and fake physics, vote open).
Tank buildingWorkingBuild mode, build menu, floors, walls, sloped armour, corners, track housings, stairs, engine, control panels, turrets, doors, decor, and the design library with sharing. Building costs materials outside the workshops. Houses are built with the same system.
MultiplayerIn progressOnline play through Steam: host, find and join, with a main menu, loading screen and settings menu. A second player can ride, climb and drive. Not yet: a server browser (on the way), building as the second player, a full test with several real players.
CombatWorkingTurrets built onto the tank, manned at their control panel or firing by themselves at enemies the antenna finds. Five kinds of ammo. Factions decide who is an enemy.
Tank damageIn progressEvery piece has health, breaks at 0 and stops working. Real holes in any mesh, shaped by the shot. Not yet: a damaged look on the tank's own pieces. The idea.
RepairWorkingThe welding torch (hold R) repairs pieces and armour, and wears out. A destroyed piece costs its materials again. The idea.
CraftingIn progressRaw materials into engineering supplies, and a craftable welding torch. Needs testing by hand and more recipes.

NPC tanksIn progressThe first enemy: drives at your tank, circles it and shoots. A tank spawner places saved designs as enemies. Next: a proper enemy tank design (student task).

Next up

  1. Students build a proper player tank and a proper enemy tank in the workshop, and a loot spawner.
  2. The test level: design desert buildings, place a village and the road to the next one, and put enemies on the way.
  3. Try money, shops and quests by hand, and make the first real quests with the Quest Maker.
  4. Close the votes: simulated or fake-physics tank, how serious the game is, and what kind of map.
