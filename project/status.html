Where we are

What is built and working today, by milestone. Updated every week. Last updated: 2026 Nov 8.

Milestone 1: a working prototype in greybox

One test level: from one village to the next, getting attacked on the way. Everything is a placeholder. Nothing is pretty and nothing is polished; everything is there to test. The point is to get a sense of what it would be like to play this in a game world, and then to ask ourselves and others: is this game worthy of existing, or is our time better used elsewhere?

9 of 17 parts working, 7 in progress74%

PartStatusNotes
PlayersWorkingOur own character, built on the GASP character: running, jumping, crouching, climbing. Footsteps sound like the ground you are on. A hotbar for what you hold, grips that hold items properly, encumbrance, and a crew headset with a head lamp.
ParkourWorkingClimb and vault on any mesh, also onto a tank that is driving.
Tank controlsWorkingPress E at any control panel to drive: W/S, A/D. One driver per tank. Tracks, engine gears and sounds that follow the driving. The fake-physics tank won the vote.
Tank buildingWorkingBuild mode, build menu, floors, walls, sloped armour, corners, track housings, stairs, engine, control panels, turrets, doors, decor, and the design library with sharing. Building costs materials outside the workshops. Houses are built with the same system.
MultiplayerIn progressOnline play through Steam: host, find and join, with a main menu, loading screen and settings menu. Hosting opens the open-world map. A second player can ride, climb and drive; loot, broken pieces, crushed plants and barrels look the same for everyone. Not yet: a server browser, building as the second player, a full test with several real players. Ten small multiplayer tasks are up as code commissions.
CombatWorkingTurrets built onto the tank, manned at their control panel or firing by themselves at enemies the antenna finds. Five kinds of ammo. Factions decide who is an enemy. A hand weapon (the blunderbuss), explosive barrels, and ambushes.
Tank damageIn progressEvery piece has health, breaks at 0 and stops working. Real holes in the tank's own armour, shaped by the shot, now in real fights. Not yet: repairing holes tested by hand, and holes in multiplayer. The idea.
RepairWorkingThe welding torch (hold R) repairs pieces and armour, and wears out. A destroyed piece costs its materials again. The idea.
CraftingIn progressRaw materials into engineering supplies, and a craftable welding torch. Needs testing by hand and more recipes.
InventoryWorkingA backpack and the tank's storage, items to pick up, drop and move.
LootWorkingDestroyed pieces and enemy tanks drop some of their materials. Loot spawners (by Mikkel) fill crates and piles and refill them. Tanks carry loadouts from loot lists.
NPC tanksIn progressThe first enemy: drives at your tank, circles it and shoots. A tank spawner places saved designs as enemies, with a loadout, and ambush areas set them off. Next: a proper enemy tank design (student task).
NPC engineersNot started
NPC civiliansIn progressPeople you place in a level: named or with a random name. Walkers walk around and sit on benches. Not yet: reacting to the players or the tank.
NPC quest giverIn progressQuests with steps, shared crew quests and secret personal quests, a quest giver and the Quest Maker tool. Named people can list the quests they give; the talk menu that offers them is next. The idea.
NPC shopkeeperWorkingMoney (power crystals) and shops with their own lists and stock. Otto Kettleby runs the general store in Newbie Town. No counter or furniture yet. The idea.
The test levelIn progressThe open-world map: Newbie Town with its shop, Levelup Road with an ambush, and Highlevel Hill. Next: the second village, and more on the way.

Next up

  1. Play the whole road: from Newbie Town past the ambush, with real players, and see what is fun and what is missing.
  2. A talk menu for people (talk, trade, quests), and the tank's voice in real fights.
  3. Students build a proper player tank and a proper enemy tank in the workshop, and pick up the new multiplayer code commissions.
  4. Bjørn's open bugs, and close the last votes: how serious the game is, and what kind of map.
