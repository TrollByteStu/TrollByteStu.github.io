Pitch: VR Arcade "JellyTetrageddon"

A Wobbly, Bizarre VR Tetris Shooter Experience!

Game Concept:

In JellyTetrageddon, you’re thrown into an intergalactic clean-up operation gone horribly wrong. After a failed bioweapon experiment, space stations have been overrun with jiggly, flailing, alien corpse-blobs that refuse to stay put. Your mission? Blast, nudge, and wrangle these floppy horrors into perfectly stacked Tetris formations before they pile up and consume your outpost!

But beware—the queue of spectators watching your struggle isn’t just there to cheer you on. Oh no. They’re voting on your next nightmare—whether it’s a gravity shift, a slippery goo storm, or the arrival of a hungry space predator ready to feast on your jello-like stockpile of alien bodies!

Core Gameplay:

  • Physics-Based Shooting: Armed with a custom-built blaster (assembled pre-game from a selection of weird sci-fi gun parts), you shoot, nudge, and push the wobbly blobs into place.
  • Tetris Meets Chaos: These alien corpses are NOT cooperative—they flop, slide, and jostle unpredictably. Landing a perfect line is as much luck as it is skill!
  • Spectator Interference: The queue watching your game can vote on what hazards spawn next. Maybe they summon a space leech that eats your carefully stacked bodies, or activate a reverse gravity event that sends everything flying!
  • Time Limit Madness: You’ve got five minutes to score as many completed "Tetris lines" as possible before the station is overrun with jelly-flopping madness!

Unique Features:

🟠 Wobbly Ragdoll Physics: These alien blobs bounce, jiggle, and behave in completely unpredictable ways!
🟢 Spectator-Controlled Mayhem: The crowd can choose hazards, making every round different and hilarious!
🔵 Weapon Customization: Before the game starts, you assemble a crazy sci-fi gun from different mods—some make it sticky, some shock the blobs, and others vacuum them into place!
🟣 Procedural Chaos: Every match is a new disaster, with changing physics events, unpredictable obstacles, and random alien floppiness!

Win & Lose Conditions:

✅ Winning: Stack as many complete lines as possible before time runs out!
❌ Losing: If the pile reaches the danger zone or a spectator-spawned hazard destroys your progress, it's GAME OVER!

Why It’s Perfect for Conventions:

✔ Fast-Paced (5-Minute Sessions): Easy to jump in, fail hilariously, and laugh!
✔ Hilarious Physics: The unpredictable wobbliness guarantees fun moments for both players and spectators!
✔ Audience Interaction: The crowd gets involved, making it a shared experience instead of just watching someone play VR alone!

Summary:

A goofy, physics-based Tetris-shooter where you blast jiggly alien corpses into a stack, fight against chaotic physics and spectator-controlled hazards, and survive the madness for a high score! Perfect for quick, replayable, convention-friendly VR fun!
