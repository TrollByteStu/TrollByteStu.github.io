Pitch: VR Arcade "Goblin Jetpack Joust"

Genre: VR Arcade Party Battle
Players: 2-4 multiplayer
Duration: 5 minutes

Premise:
It’s chaos in the goblin scrapyard arena! You and up to three other goblin daredevils strap on unstable jetpacks and grab fuel tanks to power your crazy flight maneuvers. But be warned—every jump ignites any fuel spilled behind you, turning the battlefield into a fiery gauntlet. The last goblin standing wins!

Gameplay:

  • Pick up fuel tanks scattered around the arena to power your jetpack.

  • Every time you jump or boost, you leave behind a trail of burning fuel, creating hazardous zones.

  • Crash into opponents, trick them into flying into fire, or use the environment to outmaneuver them.

  • Larger goblins move slower but can tank more hits, while smaller goblins are faster but fragile.

  • The last goblin not turned into a crispy burger wins!

Crowd/Queue Participation Idea:

  • Spectators can bet on goblins before the match starts. Winning bets get leaderboard points or a reward system.

  • Spectators can activate environmental hazards like oil spills, wind gusts, or explosive barrels.

  • Eliminated players can act as ‘ghost goblins’ who drop fire traps to mess with survivors.
