MEAT MACHINES

A silly Factorio by day. A silly Plants-vs-Zombies by night. Same monsters.

The hook

Everyone fears the AI. Here — you're the AI.

You run a medieval war-factory. Your workforce is monsters and captured soldiers bolted into machine costumes. They churn out materiel all day. At night, they remember what they are.

The pitch, honestly

Two games, bolted together at dusk

Phase 1 · Day
🏭 A silly Factorio
  • Costume a monster, wire it onto a line
  • Optimise throughput & supply chains
  • Keep disguises up; dodge the Inspector
  • Bank resources, build defences
Phase 2 · Night
🧟 A silly Plants-vs-Zombies
  • Your day-built traps are your "plants"
  • The waves are other players' machines
  • Your machines attack their lanes too
  • Everyone is gardener AND horde
Why the halves are one system

The better your day, the bigger your night.

🏭 great day→🧟 hungry army→😬 aimed at friends

Both games share one body of monsters. Every ounce of daytime success is a fully-armed night threat. The best factory-master is, by dawn, the person nobody wants to sleep next to.

The gag — and its cost

Work them hard, and the disguise slips.

Cold & idle = convincing, low output. Red-lining = obviously alive, high output. You want them hot for the quota — except when the tax-man is on the floor.

The metronome the day dances to

The King's Inspector

The Overlord's auditor. Find a flaw and he taxes you to the bone or confiscates the machine — there goes your best night-hunter. He does rounds: throttle to a cold hum when he's near, crank back to red when he leaves. And he's an LLM you must talk past.

The signature moment — the bluff

Talk your loom past the Inspector

Inspector

This loom is warm. Looms are not warm. Explain.

"Loom"

Friction, your honour. I've been weaving. Vigorously. All night. For the quota.

Inspector

It has no thread on it. …It just blinked. Forty crowns, or I take it.

"Loom"

Report favourably and I'll show my other new features tonight. At your house.

…and by night

The costume comes off.

The roster you optimised for output is now your tower-defence army — and your neighbour's wave. Tuned for production = clumsy hunter. Lean & hungry = wrecks rivals, skimps quota.

The dark joke underneath

What are you even making?

The Adventurers' Guild ships you defeated monsters & prisoners. You costume them and work them to churn war-materiel for the King. The "AI efficiency revolution" is just monsters in cardboard — who only get up at night.

Your sharpest question

If one player's always better — how is it co-op?

Player A

Smelt

Scrap → ingots

Player B

Forge

Ingots → parts

Player C

Assemble

Parts → war-goods

Player D

Pack

Crate the tribute

A shared supply chain, not a leaderboard. No one hits quota alone; overproducing just bottlenecks your neighbour; the weakest link is everyone's problem — so propping up the straggler is how the strong players win.

The bones (all invented, all yours)

The rules that hold it together

  • Leash & dawn-seize — machines tether to your Furnace; stranded at dawn = scrap a rival can salvage.
  • Sigil-scent spare-list — they never eat their master; pay upkeep to spare an ally.
  • "The Quota" — hit the King's finished-goods target over ~5 nights. Variants: Last Foreman, Endless.
  • Eaten → you become the AI — an LLM runs your factory as the Ghost; teammates can relight your Furnace to revive you.
