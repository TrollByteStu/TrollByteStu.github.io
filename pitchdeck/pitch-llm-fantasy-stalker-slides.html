FANTASY STALKER

Respectable shopkeeper by day. The thing in the fog by night. And the paper knows more each morning.

The hook

Jack the Ripper — but everyone's a dwarf in a top hat.

You're a legitimate business owner who only feels alive when he kills. You need to do wrong to get the energy to do right. A comedy of manners with a body count.

The spine of the whole game

You tighten your own noose

01

Fuel, and a thrill you can't skip.

02
Grow strong

Energy back; shops & body sharpen.

03

A weapon's mark, a dropped scissor.

04

Cops print the clue, seal your streets.

05
Grow weak

Locked districts starve your night.

…so you must kill more — more traces, more sealed streets. Every murder is your fuel and your rope.

Two phases — the camera is the horror

Safe by day. Hunted by night.

Phase 1 · RTS overhead
🏙️ The shopkeeper

2-to-8 businesses from a calm god's-eye view. In control… and complicit.

Phase 2 · first person
🌫️ Two hours in the fog

Up close, you can be hurt. Later, only bosses & cops are worth a kill.

As the cops seal districts, your safe daytime board literally shrinks, night by night.

Why you can't just stop

Two meters, pulling opposite ways

Energy

Drains if you abstain. Skip too long and you wither.

Heat

Rises with each kill; cools over ~3 nights of lying low.

The smart play is to lie low — but Energy won't let you. "I should skip tonight… I can't skip tonight." You kill because you're out of road.

Every clue sourced from what you actually did — MO, shop, coat. The net feels earned, never random.
