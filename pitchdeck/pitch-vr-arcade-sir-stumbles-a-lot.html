Pitch: VR Arcade "Sir Stumbles-a-Lot"

A VR Dungeon-Crawling Disaster Simulator

The Premise

You are the clever rogue reluctantly hired by a band of big, dumb, visor-down knights to guide them through a deadly dungeon. The problem? They don’t listen.

These clueless, armor-clad jocks will charge ahead blindly, smashing into walls, stepping on traps, and generally making your life a medieval nightmare. You’ll need to navigate the dungeon from above, using a grappling hook system to swing between ceiling fixtures, disarm traps, and occasionally let fate take its course when a knight gets too annoying.

The goal? Get at least one knight to the treasure.
The challenge? The fewer knights survive, the higher your score. But if all of them die, you fail completely.

Gameplay Overview

🎯 Your Role – The Rogue Guide

  • Swing Between Ceiling Points: Use your grappling hook to quickly zip between chandeliers, chains, and wooden beams, avoiding the knightly chaos below.
  • Disable Traps: Interact with pressure plates, tripwires, and swinging axes before the knights walk right into them.
  • Manipulate the Environment: Drop planks over pitfalls, trigger fake treasure chests, or rearrange obstacles to funnel the knights where you want them.
  • Decide Who Lives (and Who "Accidentally" Dies): Some knights are really annoying. Maybe you forget to warn them about the spike trap. Oops.

🏃 The Knights – Walking Disasters

  • Run Headfirst into Everything: If they hit a wall, they stumble, curse, and waddle in another random direction.
  • Trigger Every Trap Possible: They will step on every pressure plate, poke every suspicious wall, and kick every chest (even the obvious mimics).
  • Complain Constantly: Fully voice-acted grunts, insults, and panic screams make it hilarious to watch their self-inflicted misfortune.

⚔️ Obstacles & Traps

  • Classic Pitfalls – Some knights fall, others trip and take more knights with them.
  • Swinging Guillotines – Perfect for cutting the team down to a more manageable size.
  • Rolling Boulders – Watch knights sprint in terror while one unlucky guy tries to fight it.
  • Fake Treasure Chests – The classic mimic ambush.
  • Illusion Walls – You see them, they don’t. Guess who runs straight in?

Why It's Fun

✅ Chaotic & Hilarious – Watching a team of overconfident idiots meet their doom is endlessly entertaining.
✅ Unique VR Locomotion – Grappling around the dungeon feels amazing and keeps you above the chaos.
✅ Dynamic Puzzle-Solving – No two runs play the same! Adapt on the fly as the knights mess everything up.
✅ Slapstick Medieval Comedy – The knights are fully voice-acted, reacting to every mishap with Monty Python-style wit.

Summary

Sir Stumbles-a-Lot is a 6-minute VR dungeon run where players must outsmart a squad of medieval jocks, using grappling traversal to guide them just enough to survive—while laughing at their constant blunders. It's part strategy, part slapstick, and all chaos.

Will you be the genius tactician who saves the knights?
…Or the smug rogue who enjoys the carnage a little too much? 😈
