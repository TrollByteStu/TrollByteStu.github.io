Pitch: VR Arcade Co-op "Tick Tock Tinkers"

Game Overview

Tick Tock Tinkerers is a fast-paced, cooperative multiplayer VR game where players take on the role of steampunk gnomes piloting floating balloon baskets inside the colossal gears of The Grand Machine—a massive clock tower on the verge of failure. With only five minutes before the bells ring, players must scramble to repair the intricate clockwork while fending off mischievous gremlins determined to sabotage their efforts.

Players must coordinate, grab scattered gears, and place them correctly before time runs out. But it’s not that simple—gravity is against them! Their balloons slowly sink, and the only way to stay afloat is to smack their imp assistant, who obediently pumps the bellows to keep them airborne. Meanwhile, grappling hooks allow for quick lateral movement, and a selection of weapons—including shotguns, flamethrowers, and turret guns—help deal with the relentless gremlin hordes.

The more repairs completed, the higher the team’s score. Fail to meet the minimum threshold, and The Grand Machine grinds to a halt, silencing the bells forever—and taking the players with it.

Key Features

🔹 Unique Co-op Gameplay – Work together to repair the machine while battling pesky gremlins. Coordination is key!
🔹 VR Motion & Tools – Hover using bellows-powered flight, grapple across the tower, and use extendable grabbers to manipulate gears.
🔹 Chaotic Enemy Interference – Gremlins don’t just attack; they taunt, sabotage, and build ridiculous contraptions to mess with you.
🔹 Diverse Weapons & Tools – Switch between repair tools and weapons like flamethrowers and turret guns on the fly.
🔹 High Replayability – Procedurally scattered gears, unpredictable gremlin behavior, and varying levels of machine decay ensure no two rounds are alike.
🔹 Score-Based Success – The more repairs completed before time runs out, the higher the team’s score—and the louder the bells ring!

Conclusion

Tick Tock Tinkerers delivers high-energy, teamwork-driven VR chaos where players must balance speed, precision, and outright mayhem to keep The Grand Machine running. With fast five-minute rounds, dynamic team-based mechanics, and mischievous AI-driven enemies, it’s a perfect blend of challenge and comedy—ideal for VR arcades, party play, and co-op lovers.

So grab your grappling hook, smack your imp, and get to work—time is ticking!
