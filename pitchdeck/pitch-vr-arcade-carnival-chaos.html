Pitchdeck · Carnival Chaos

Pitch: VR Arcade "Carnival Chaos"

Players: Single-player Duration: 1-5 minutes

Premise:

A carnival out in the country with a bunch of fun games. Warm colors and a bunch of differently colored lights

Story

You are a kid attending a carnival and want to win the biggest prize.

Competition

The better you are at the carnival games, the more tickets you get, get as many as possible to go on the leaderbaord.

Games

  • Whack a mole
  • Ring toss
  • Balloon dart toss
  • Claw machine
  • Hammer game

Gameplay:

  • Win as many games as possible
  • Get a ton of tickets to put you on the leaderboard
