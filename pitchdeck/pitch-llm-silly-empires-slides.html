SILLY EMPIRES

History, if every myth were simply true. An LLM runs the world; gods run on followers.

The hook

Don't blend fantasy and history — believe them.

Take every culture's tallest tale at face value and crank it to eleven. A grand-strategy sandbox that's secretly a comedy about what nations think they are.

Are you a god, or a lord?

Play from below
♜ The Lord
  • Grow cities, raise armies, wage silly wars
  • Pray, tithe & build shrines for divine boons
  • Out-scheme rival lords (mostly LLM)
Play from above
✦ The God
  • Command faith, not armies — boons, omens, plagues
  • Mana = followers × devotion; starve rival gods
  • Lords are your LLM pieces to cultivate

Same map, same sim — only the camera and the verbs change. Which is why we can build both.

The engine under both modes

Gods run on followers; lords run on gods.

A god with no believers is just a rumour. A lord with no god loses the war. Followers → mana → miracles → favour → more followers. Everyone needs — and resents — everyone.

Your best idea, turned into a lesson

Build both — let players settle it

0
Shared world-sim — map, turns, empires, worship economy. Built once.
1
Two thin slices, temporary controls — rough Lord + rough God on top. Test feel, not UI.
2
Run a real beta — same scenario, half in each mode; log where players lean in / check out.
3
Ask the right questions — liked? bored when? wanted-but-couldn't? play again — which mode?
4
Decide by threshold, not vote — agree the bar before testing; let the numbers rule.
The teachable bit — agree it up front

The decision rubric

Must-have≥ ~70% rate it fun AND it drove the best moments → build it out.
Iterate~40–70% positive → change one thing, test again.
Cut≥ ~50% bored/negative, or nobody picks it twice → drop it, guilt-free.

God-vs-lord falls out of the same rule: both pass → ship both; one → focus; neither → the fun's in the world, rework agency. Nobody has to win the argument.

The ask

A satire engine, a pantheon of LLMs — and a lesson in letting players decide.

Two students, two visions. Build both. Test. Ship what's fun.
