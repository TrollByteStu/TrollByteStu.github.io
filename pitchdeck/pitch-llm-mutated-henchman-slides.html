MUTATED HENCHMAN

You defend a horror you'll never see. Every death brings you back more mutated — and less yourself.

The hook

Death isn't game over. It's a promotion.

A voice in your skull pays you to keep its coffin sealed. Each time you fall it drags you back with +10% mutation — power you buy by handing your body to something that isn't you.

The shape of a run

Get paid to keep the lid shut

Phase A

Defend

Waves come for the coffin. Every kill pays.

Phase B

Spend

Guns, gear, and surgery — on yourself.

Loop

Escalate

Harder waves, deeper upgrades, the whispering.

Fail

Breach

Coffin cracked = you lose, and finally see the boss.

The mechanic it's all built on

Every death is a promotion you didn't want.

90% mutated

You steer 10%. The rest is a hungrier thing in your skin.

Power and control sit on opposite ends of the same slider.

Your employer, who is also the horror

A voice you obey and never meet

"Carry me to the house with the small chairs. Set me in the middle of the room. You need not stay."

You get paid. You buy an upgrade. It was a good day.

Next morning: the small chairs are gone. The walls are not white anymore. Nobody made you look.

The horror is never in your face

You're not scared — until you understand.

No jump-scares. The master is polite, the jobs simple, the pay good. The horror is the arithmetic of what you've done — assembled in your own head, one contract too late. You chose every step. You'd probably take the next one too.

(From the two students who stayed late — bring it back to the whole team.)

Your smartest production idea — kept

LLM teammates, alive but cheap

Don't call the model mid-firefight. Generate each merc's lines up front as banks; pick one at runtime. Same character, no latency.

Critical

"Ha! Twenty percent of that was me!"

The lesson: author-time generation, run-time selection. Build content once, cheaply — not every frame.

The thing we genuinely didn't settle

What world is this? Take your pick.

A · Gothic Horde

Besieged bazaar of the dead; blades, bone, grim & grand.

B · Sunless Temple

Ritual, fog, firelight. The most overtly eldritch.

C · Gun-Mercs

Grimy near-future; rifles, cybersurgery, "cheap mercs."

The systems don't care which. My lean: C with A's grimness — and a perfect thing to hand to a playtest.

The ask

A new mechanic: buy power with your own control.

Plus two techniques worth stealing: bark banks & horror-by-realisation.
