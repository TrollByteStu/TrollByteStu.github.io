Pitch: VR Arcade "Monster Munchies"

Cook fast. Cook weird. Feed the fiends.

The Core Fantasy

You’re the sole chef at The Midnight Diner, a cursed culinary hotspot that serves only the grossest, weirdest foods to a parade of impatient monsters. Think Overcooked meets WarioWare in VR — players grab, slap, squeeze, slice, cook, and serve grotesque ingredients to make monstrosities like:

  • Ghoul Hand Sandwiches

  • Eyeball & Tentacle Ramen

  • Werewolf Liver Latte

  • Vampire Bloodwurst in a Garlic Bun (hold the garlic!)

  • Cursed Taco with Slime Sauce

Gameplay Loop (5-Min Demo)

  1. Customer Enters – A goofy monster pops up at the diner counter with a speech bubble order. They tap their claws or moan for speed.

  2. Grab Ingredients – Player must quickly select the right body parts, sauces, weird side items from the pantry or fridge (doors slam shut if you dawdle!).

  3. Prepare & Combine – Mash, blend, roast, zap with an unholy microwave, wrap with bandages — whatever the recipe needs.

  4. Deliver – Slap the food on a plate, ring the dinner bell, and see the monster’s reaction (satisfied? vomit rainbows? explodes with joy?)

  5. Repeat – Difficulty ramps up with more ingredients, time pressure, or trick orders ("Hold the soul!").

Visual Style

Cartoonish, exaggerated 3D – like Job Simulator + Monster Prom. Big googly eyes, rubbery animations, and slime physics. Everything squelches, jiggles, or farts when touched.

Spectator Mode / Party Chaos

Let non-VR spectators at the booth interfere or assist using a touch panel or phone connection:

QR Code Party Mode

Spectators scan a QR code to join as “Kitchen Gremlins.” They vote on modifiers like:

  • “Next customer wants EVERYTHING with eyeballs.”

  • “All slime ingredients are slippery now!”

  • “Chef's hands are now giant claws!”

Votes apply in real-time.

What Works / What Could Improve

✅ Simple loop: Easy for players to understand in under 30 seconds.
✅ Fun to watch: Wacky ingredients, silly physics, loud reactions.
✅ Spectator engagement: Adds showboating and shared chaos.
✅ Expandable: More monsters, new tools, ingredients, seasonal themes.

⚠️ Needs:

  • A clear win/fail condition. Maybe a timer with a goal: “Serve 5 happy customers in 5 minutes.”

  • Consider “combo orders” (2 items) to ramp up difficulty in the last minute.

  • Possibly too much multitasking for very young players unless you use visual guides/ghost hands.
