BOILERHEART

The thing you fight to keep alive isn't you. It's the two-storey thinking war-machine you live inside.

The one-line twist

Move the health bar off the hero and onto the machine.

Hull, boiler pressure, coolant, fuel, oil, crew nerves — those are the stats you bleed and defend. You're just the crew keeping the beast running to the next town.

The world

An empire at war — and machines that learned to think.

You crew a hulking land-dreadnought: part tank, part home, part cranky old soul. Cross the frontier, barter in towns, take missions, survive battles, keep it alive.

Where the AI actually lives

Three LLMs, three different jobs

🛞
Character voice

The Machine

A rustbucket prima donna with moods and vices — gets hooked on premium oils. Spoil it and it soars; ration it and it sulks.

🧑‍🔧
Living NPCs

The Crew

Named people you can talk to. Loyalties, secrets — and on some runs, a traitor aboard.

🎲
Procedural GM

The Game Master

Fed loose story fragments; weaves them into one coherent mission on the fly. No two runs alike.

The killer demo — fragments in

Hand the GM a fistful of fragments…

ORDER exfiltrate a spy TWIST a traitor is aboard SNAG the spy won't come willingly AMBUSH raiders on the way home PLACE return to the FOB

…and it welds them into a story ▶

…mission out
Command signals the Dowager: pull an imperial spy out of an occupied town by dawn. But she won't board — her brother's in the work-camp and she won't leave without him. Meanwhile a traitor on your deck has leaked the route, setting a raider ambush in the canyon back to the FOB. Get everyone home… and you still might not know who sold you out.

Underlined = the fragments. Swap any one, and the mission re-weaves.

Survival, relocated

Maintenance is the survival system

Staying alive means engineering. Repair & upgrade are the puzzles; town supplies keep the needles out of the red.

For the staff room

Why teach this one?

  • Character voice — personality + state
  • Conversational NPCs — memory + a hidden role
  • Procedural GM — structured output from fragments
  • Each role demos on its own
  • Students can own one module
  • "The tank talks" sells the tech instantly
  • Starts as text — no engine needed to prove it
If we greenlight it

First slice — one term, honest scope

  • One town · one talking machine · 6 live stats (incl. Sanity) · a barter screen
  • GM assembles one mission from ~6–8 fragments (and re-weaves on swap)
  • Three crew to talk to; one is the traitor this run
  • One maintenance puzzle standing in for the survival layer
  • Text-first — art & 3D come after the loop is fun
The ask
