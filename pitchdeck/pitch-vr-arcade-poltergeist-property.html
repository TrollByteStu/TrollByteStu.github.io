Pitch: VR Arcade "Poltergeist Property"

“Selling a haunted house was never this wild! With Poltergeist Property, you’ll face mischievous ghosts and hilarious sabotage tactics that’ll keep you on your toes. Here’s what you can expect:”

You are a realtor trying to sell a haunted house. Customers are coming to see the property, and you need to keep them distracted so they dont notice the ghosts. At the end of the time if a customer is left, he will buy it. If they have all been spooked, you loose.

Features List:

  • Grabbing Power: Grab anything, from furniture to ghosts, and use it to keep things in check!
  • Furniture Fiasco: Ghosts love to move things around—can you catch them in time and restore order?
  • Spooky Mirrors: Catch ghosts haunting your mirrors with a quick grab of a cloth before the customers notice!
  • Distraction Mania: Use radios, noisy bells, and pianos to draw attention away from the ghostly noises.
  • Ghostly Resistance: Some ghosts fight back! Grab them and try to toss them out before they cause more trouble.

Comical and silly ghosts plague your big opportunity, they will sabotage your big sale in any way they can.

Controls:

  • VR Walk or teleport: Controllers lets you walk or teleport as in most other games.
  • VR Grab: Point at something and hold grab button on the controller, to grab and move that item.
  • Activate: Some things cannot be flung around, only activated. Point at them and grab to turn on a radio or play a piano.
  • Yeet: Ghosts are elastic and stretchy, and will resist if you grab them and fling them out the window or door. Once the elastic snaps, physics happens.
  • UI: Glowing text and arrows clearly shows when ghosts have moved something, and where you need to move them back to. They will snap back into the original place.
  • Stampede: Panic is contagious, if customers see 3 other customers in panic, they will panic themselves. Close doors and guide them out of the house before the others notice.
