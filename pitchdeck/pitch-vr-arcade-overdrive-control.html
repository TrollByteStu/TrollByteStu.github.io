Pitchdeck · Overdrive Control

Pitch: VR Arcade "Overdrive Control"

Players: Single-player Duration: 1-5 minutes

Premise:

The control room in a big dystopian factory in the 20th century. Very dead and depressing colors.

Story

You were a homeless man living in the 20th century, you find a newspaper promising you a job at a factory, you accept and get sent into the control room of the factory, your job is to keep everything working.

Gameplay:

  • The more you play, the harder it gets.
  • Keep everything working.
  • Keep track of everything you have to do.
