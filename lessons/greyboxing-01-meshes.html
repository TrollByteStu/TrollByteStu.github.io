Greyboxing Course 01: Static, Dynamic & Modular Meshes

Before making a big pretty world, you test the concepts with plain grey boxes. No one wants to spend 3 days building a large pretty level, and then test it to find you have 2 minutes of fun puzzles and obstacles and 20 minutes of boring running. You want to test before you put the calories into making it pretty.

This tutorial teaches you the basics of blocking out or greyboxing your level so you can test it: the different ways to do it, and how it is saved in your project. Good basic knowledge.
