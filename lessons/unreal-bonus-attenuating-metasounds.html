Unreal Bonus: Attenuating MetaSounds

Attenuation is when something stops a sound in various ways.

The sound source is blocked or partially blocked from where you are.

Direction. If the sound is to your left, the volume is slightly lower on your right, and there is a slight delay.

Across a forest or swamp, higher piched notes are lost faster than lower pitched notes by foliage and moisture.

In a place with big hard surfaces like walls or cliffs there is echo as the sound reverbs off the surfaces.

Unreal simulates these fairly well, while still keeping it simple enough for us mooks to understand and use.
