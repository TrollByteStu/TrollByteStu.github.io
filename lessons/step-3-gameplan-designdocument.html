Step 3: Gameplan/DesignDocument

Lastly you create the plan that the game will be built after.

Ideally these are so well documented that you can give it to any developer and they would make the exact same game. In reality its a rough plan, but it tells various parts of the team what they should work on and work towards.

There are small simple games and big complex games, so no unified standard to how big or detailed it should be exists. Bigger than "Make good game" and smaller than a one year read. The end result should be described as well as can be, the "feel" and the various steps needed, preferably broken into workgroup tasks.
