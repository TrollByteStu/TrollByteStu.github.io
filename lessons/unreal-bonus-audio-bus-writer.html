Unreal Bonus: Audio Bus Writer

A bus is a way to pass signals from one place to another.

If you set up your sounds in unreal to be sent through a bus, you can apply effects to many many sounds at once, and more importantly, you can turn effects up and down on all sounds at the same time for almost no CPU cost.
