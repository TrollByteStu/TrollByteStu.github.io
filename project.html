BOILERHEART II: Cranky Engines

An open-world crew sandbox. Your crew lives inside a thinking war-tank raised on poetry and opera, in a post-apocalyptic world of factions. Made by teachers and students at Specialisterne STU Denmark, in Unreal Engine, and meant to keep growing for years.
